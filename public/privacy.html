Privacy Policy

01

Overview

IronMap ("we", "our", or "the app") is a gym training and daily habit tracking app. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

The short version: IronMap stores your gym data locally on your device. We do not require an account. We do not sell your data. We do not run ads. Core training features work without an internet connection. Two features send data off your device when you use them: communities (your check-ins) and the AI Coach (the training data needed to answer you).

03

Community Features

If you join a community using a community code, the following data is synced to our backend (Supabase) to enable the community feed:

  • Your anonymous device ID
  • Your chosen display name
  • Your daily check-in completion data (items completed, streak count)
  • Optional short posts you choose to share with the community

This data is associated with your device ID only — not your name, email, or any other identity. Other community members can see your display name, streak, and check-in completion status.

Community data is voluntary. You can use all workout tracking and personal check-in features without joining any community. Leaving a community removes your data from the community feed.
04

AI Coach

When you send the coach a message, IronMap builds the smallest slice of your training data needed to answer it — which can include your logged workout history, personal records, per-exercise effort ratings, training goal, body weight, age bracket, gym equipment, and recovery data you log (including self-reported condition ratings) — and sends it, with your message, to our backend and on to Anthropic, the AI provider that generates the reply.

  • Data is sent only when you actively use the coach — never in the background.
  • Per Anthropic's commercial API terms, your data is not used to train AI models. Anthropic may retain API inputs briefly (up to ~30 days) for abuse monitoring.
  • We do not store your chat transcripts on our servers. Transcripts live on your device; sharing one with us (feedback export) is always your action.
  • We store per-device usage counts to enforce the coach's fair-use limits.
05

Apple HealthKit (iOS)

On iOS with Apple Watch, IronMap requests read access to heart rate data during workouts. This data is:

  • Displayed live on your Apple Watch during active sessions
  • Saved to Apple Health as a workout record when your session ends
  • Never transmitted to IronMap's servers
  • Never used for advertising or analytics

IronMap does not write data to HealthKit other than workout records you explicitly complete. You can revoke HealthKit access at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → IronMap.

06

Purchases

Pro purchases are processed by Apple (App Store). We never see your payment details. We use RevenueCat to validate purchases and unlock Pro across reinstalls; RevenueCat stores your purchase history against an anonymous ID.

On builds distributed through the Solana dApp Store, an optional Pro unlock can be purchased via the Solana wallet you connect; the transaction occurs on the public Solana blockchain and is inherently public.

07

Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

We use the following third-party services:

  • Supabase — backend for community features, the AI Coach relay, and coach usage limits.
  • Anthropic — AI provider that generates coach replies (see AI Coach).
  • RevenueCat — purchase validation for Pro (see Purchases).

We do not use analytics SDKs, advertising networks, or third-party tracking.

08

Data Retention & Deletion

Local data (workout logs, check-ins, settings) is stored on your device and remains until you delete the app or use the "Delete all data" option in Settings.

Community data on our servers is retained while you are a member of a community. When you leave a community, your check-in data for that community is removed from the feed. You can request full deletion of all server-side data associated with your device ID by contacting us.

Coach usage counts are retained as rolling aggregates. The "Delete all data" option in Settings, or a deletion request by email, removes all server-side data associated with your device ID, including coach usage records.

09

Children's Privacy

IronMap is designed for users aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided data through IronMap, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.
